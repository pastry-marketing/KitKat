KITKAT BRIDGE - AUTOMATIC START
================================

You only need to set this up once on each user's PC.

1. Open "Start KitKat Bridge.bat" and sign in once.
2. When the bridge says it is connected, close that window.
3. Double-click "Install Automatic Start.bat".

After setup:

- KitKat Bridge starts automatically when you sign in to Windows.
- No black CMD window needs to stay open.
- A KitKat Bridge icon appears near the Windows clock.
- Double-click the icon to open KitKat.
- Right-click it to view the log, restart, or stop the bridge.
- You may move or delete the downloaded ZIP folder after installation.

Incogniton must still be open when you want KitKat to control profiles.

To disable automatic start, double-click "Remove Automatic Start.bat".
